#### Project Description

When Wendy Fry, a reporter with NBC 7 San Diego, requested data for a story about street sweeping, the city sent her a dump from their dispatch system's GPS logs, a dozen files with several million records. Civic Knowledge, working under its nonprofit[ San Diego Regional Data Library](<https://www.sandiegodata.org/>), processed the records to determine which streets had been swept. By merging the reduced data with records of traffic tickets, we determined where residents had been ticketed for parking on a street that was not swept, [to support a series of NBC 7 stories and television segments](<http://www.nbcsandiego.com/investigations/If-Streets-Arent-Swept-Street-Sweeping-Parking-Violations-Still-Need-to-Be-Paid-City-329625761.html>).
